# User Requirements

## 1. Guest / Attendee
- The Guest shall be able to view the event registration page using a public slug.
- The Guest shall be able to register for an event by providing their personal details (Name, Email, Phone, Organization, etc.).
- The Guest shall be able to upload a photo of themselves during registration for identification purposes.
- The Guest shall be able to select a ticket tier (Main, Overflow) if applicable.
- The Guest shall be able to receive an automated email confirmation upon successful registration or approval.
- The Guest shall be prevented from registering multiple times with the same email address.
- The Guest shall be informed if the event is full or if they are placed on a waitlist.

## 2. Administrator (Organizer)
- The Administrator shall be able to log in to the system securely using email and password.
- The Administrator shall be able to create, update, and manage event details (Title, Description, Location, Dates, Banner).
- The Administrator shall be able to configure registration quotas for "Main" and "Overflow" slots.
- The Administrator shall be able to enable or disable public registration and set an automatic close date/time.
- The Administrator shall be able to view a dashboard with real-time statistics on registrations (counts for Main, Overflow, Pending, Approved).
- The Administrator shall be able to view a list of all registrations with filtering options (by status, slot type).
- The Administrator shall be able to approve or reject individual registrations.
- The Administrator shall be able to perform bulk actions (Approve Selected, Delete Selected) on registrations.
- The Administrator shall be able to export registration data to CSV format.
- The Administrator shall be able to export attendee badges/list to PDF format.
- The Administrator shall be able to soft-delete registrations.

## 3. Check-in Staff
- The Check-in Staff shall be able to verify an attendee's identity using their ticket code or name.
- The Check-in Staff shall be able to view the attendee's photo during check-in for visual verification.
- The Check-in Staff shall be able to mark an attendee as "Checked In".
- The Check-in Staff shall be able to undo a check-in if made in error.
- The Check-in Staff shall be able to view the current check-in status of an attendee.
